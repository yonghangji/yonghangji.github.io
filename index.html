Job Market Paper

Worker Selection and Efficiency (with Allen Vong)

Abstract: We study an employment relationship in which a manager repeatedly selects one worker from a group to perform a task subject to moral hazard, while others rest. We explicitly construct an equilibrium that achieves efficient worker performance whenever an efficient equilibrium exists. Our equilibrium features a simple and intuitive review strategy: the manager reviews a worker's performance only when the worker is selected again after a rest period. This review strategy effectively disciplines workers and ensures efficiency. We also extend our analysis to settings with heterogeneous workers and discuss the implications for organizational design.
